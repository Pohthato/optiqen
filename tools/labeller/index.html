    Court points

    Pause on a frame where the court is clear. Click the four corners of the singles court — the name to click is shown below — then press Propose the rest and fix any point by dragging it.

    Yellow points are yours; grey ones are proposals. A magnifier follows the cursor for precise clicks.

      Hits

      Press Find hits once. Then N jumps to the next likely hit: step with , . to the frame where racket meets shuttle and click the shot. X skips a sound that isn't a hit.

      Rallies

      Rallies are suggested from the pauses between hits. Check each one and choose who won it (as seen from the camera).

        Body pose

        Choose your player. B goes to the next hit frame and fills in the skeleton; drag any joint that is off, right-click a joint you can't see, then Enter to confirm.
